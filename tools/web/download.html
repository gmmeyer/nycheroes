NYC Heroes for Windows and Mac

The same game as in the browser, as an app. It streams the city from this site like the browser does, so it needs the internet; places you've been are kept on your computer, and play without it. Co-op works with friends in the browser too: same room codes.

Windows

Download for Windows
  1. Unzip it, and open NYC Heroes.exe in the folder.
  2. The app isn't signed yet, so Windows may say it protected your PC: click More info, then Run anyway.

64-bit Windows 10 or 11, a graphics card with OpenGL 3.3.

Mac

Download for Mac
  1. Unzip it, and move NYC Heroes to your Applications folder.
  2. The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time macOS says it can't check it. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to NYC Heroes. (Before macOS 15, Control-click the app and choose Open instead.)

macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon, or 10.15 or later on Intel. Or, in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/NYC Heroes.app"

Keys: WASD to move, the mouse to look and punch, Space to jump, F to fly, E to web-swing, M for co-op, F11 for full screen.
